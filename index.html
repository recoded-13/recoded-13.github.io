    Description §1
    I'm a biomedical engineer who works on brain-computer interfaces and neural signal processing. I like problems where the signal is weak, the noise is loud and it all has to work in real time.
    Right now I'm building the real-time neural decoder for a closed-loop BCI device for dysphagia at Ruten Inc. I'm also the data analyst on four EEG and EMG studies of posture and balance at the University of Sharjah. Before that I built a motor imagery BCI that moves a robotic hand from three EEG channels.
    Features §2
    • Real-time EEG decoding pipelines, from acquisition to classifier to actuator
    • Corticomuscular and intramuscular coherence, functional connectivity and time-frequency analysis
    • Motor imagery BCIs built on Lab Streaming Layer and deployed on a Raspberry Pi
    • Experiment design in PsychoPy, iterated over pilot rounds
    • Embedded sensing on STM32 and Arduino
    • Resting-state fMRI connectivity in SPM and CONN
